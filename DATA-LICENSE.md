# Data license

The contents of `data/` are made available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

Much of the route geometry is traced from OpenStreetMap, so this dataset is a derived database
of OpenStreetMap data and is released under the same license.

**Attribution:** route geometry © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

Station locations and current timetables are derived from Amtrak's public GTFS feed. Historical
dates, headways and notes are compiled from the sources cited in each segment's `sources` field.

If you publicly use this data, or a database derived from it, you must credit it as above and
share any adapted database under the ODbL.
